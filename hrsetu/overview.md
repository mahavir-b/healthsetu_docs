# HRSetu — people & payroll

HRSetu manages **employees**, **attendance**, **leave**, **salary**, training, BGV, and **My HR** self-service for every staff member.

## What we provide

- Employee master and workplaces
- Shift roster and attendance
- Leave requests and approvals
- Salary / payroll, statutory, full & final
- Training and background verification trackers
- Self-service: check-in, leave, documents, salary slips (as enabled)
