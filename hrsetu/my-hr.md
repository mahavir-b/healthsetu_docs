# My HR (self-service)

Every employee with a login can use **My HR** without being an HR admin.

## What you can usually do

| Action | Where |
|--------|--------|
| Check in / attendance self-service | My HR → Check-in |
| Apply for leave | My HR → Leave |
| View documents | My HR → Documents |
| View salary slip (when published) | My HR → Salary |
| Clearances | My HR → Clearances |
