# Salary & statutory

HR payroll users run **Salary**, **Statutory**, and **Full & Final** as enabled for your organisation.

## Typical monthly cycle

1. Freeze attendance and leave for the period.
2. Open **Salary** / payroll run.
3. Review exceptions, then approve per your maker-checker rules.
4. Publish payslips to **My HR** when ready.
5. Complete statutory filings outside or via your statutory screen as configured.
