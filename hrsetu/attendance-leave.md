# Shifts, attendance & leave

## Shift roster

Publish who works which shift. Staff should see their roster before the week starts. View by day, week, or month, by location or by employee; **Suggest fill** proposes staff for open shifts.

## Attendance

1. Open **Attendance**.
2. Review check-ins for the day / range.
3. Correct exceptions (missed punch, on-duty) per policy.
4. Lock or finalise as your payroll calendar requires.

</figure>

## Leave

- Employees raise leave from **My HR**.
- Managers approve from Leave / Inbox.
- Balances should be explained in your organisation policy, not only in the app.
- HR can **Record leave** on someone’s behalf and set per-type policies under **Policies**.
