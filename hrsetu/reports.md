# HR dashboard & reports

## HR dashboard

Open **Home → HR dashboard** at the start of the day. The **Today** row lists what needs action before you look at charts:

| Tile | What to do |
|------|-----------|
| Approvals waiting | Leave and payroll items waiting on HR |
| Onboarding stuck | Joiners whose onboarding checklist has stopped |
| Not cleared for check-in | Staff blocked by a pending work clearance |
| Payroll | Status of the latest payroll run |

Below that: **Attrition overview** (headcount, attrition rate, hires and separations by month) and **Compliance expiry alerts** for licences and certificates expiring within the alert window (30–180 days).

## HR reports

Open **Insights & Reports → HR reports**, choose a report, then download **CSV**.

| Report | Typical use |
|--------|-------------|
| Headcount by department | Monthly MIS |
| Joiners & leavers | Movement in the period |
| Muster roll (Form XII) | Statutory attendance register |
| Wage register (Form B) | Statutory wage register |
| Leave register | Leave taken and balance |
| OT register | Overtime hours |
| EPF ECR | Monthly EPF return file |
| ESIC challan | ESIC contribution |
| Licence expiry | Nursing / medical licences due |
| Attrition | Separations and rate |

<div class="callout callout--warn">
  Wage registers, ECR, and ESIC files contain salary and identity data. Store downloads securely and never attach them to public tickets.
</div>

## HR setup screens

Under **Administration**, HR admins maintain:

- **Workplaces** — sites and branches employees are assigned to
- **Employee setup** — departments, certifications, document templates, holiday templates, and onboarding checklists
- **Employee files** — employee documents across the organisation
- **Work clearances** — clearances an employee needs before they can check in for work

Org admins can also hide employee screens or add custom fields through **Customize functionality** (see <a href="#" data-hs-route="core/settings">Email, branding & settings</a>).
