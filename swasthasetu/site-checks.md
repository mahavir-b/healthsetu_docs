# Site safety & compliance checks

These logs show inspectors that the OHC and site safety work is done on schedule.

| Screen | What you record | How often |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|
| **First Aiders** | Trained first aiders and certificate expiry | When trained / renewed |
| **Ambulances** | Ambulances on site | Once, then when changed |
| **Ambulance Check** | Equipment and readiness checklist | Daily |
| **First Aid Box** | Box contents and issues | As per SOP |
| **Canteen Hygiene** | Hygiene inspection score (Good / Satisfactory / Needs improvement) | As per SOP |
| **Plant Inspection** | Workplace health hazards (physical, chemical, biological, psychological) and risk level | As per SOP |
| **Biomedical Waste** | Bag weights, pickup, and manifest by date (BMWM Rules 2016) | Daily |

## Monthly and yearly work

- **Monthly OHC Work** tracks completion of recurring OHC activities for the month.
- **Yearly Calendar** shows planned OHC activities across the year.

## Daily HR report

At the end of the day, prepare the **Daily HR Report**: the OHC summary sent to HR. Attach signed copies or supporting files, then dispatch it.
