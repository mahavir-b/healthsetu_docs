# SwasthaSetu — occupational health (OHC)

SwasthaSetu runs the **occupational health centre (OHC)** at a factory, plant, or campus. It keeps worker medical exams, OHC OPD, and site safety checks in one place, and gives HR the fitness decisions it needs.

## What we provide

- Employee health records linked to departments and job titles
- **Pre-employment** fitness exams, with candidate signature and HR authorization
- **Periodic medical** exams and **audiometry**, with an exam schedule driven by test requirements
- **Vaccination** records
- OHC **OPD register** for day-to-day visits and emergencies
- First aiders, first aid boxes, and ambulance checks
- Canteen hygiene, plant inspection, and **biomedical waste** logs (BMWM Rules 2016)
- Monthly OHC work, a yearly calendar, and the daily report to HR
- Trend analysis on periodic test results

## Who does what

| Role | Main screens |
|------|--------------|

| Safety / EHS | First Aiders, Ambulance Check, First Aid Box, Plant Inspection, Canteen Hygiene |

## Dashboard

The dashboard shows what needs attention today: OPD and emergencies today, medical exams due in the next 30 days, pre-employment exams pending, Form 33 pending, first-aider certificates and MOUs expiring, medicines expiring, ambulance checks, and biomedical waste waiting for pickup.

## Setup (OHC admin)

Do these in order under **Admin**:

1. **Departments** and **Job titles** — match your HR structure.
2. **Medical tests** — the tests your OHC performs, with their parameters.
3. **Test requirements** — which tests (and trainings) apply to all employees, one department, or a specific job title in a department. The exam schedule uses this.
4. **BMW agencies** — your biomedical waste collection agency and its MOU.
5. **SOPs & documents** — upload OHC SOPs so staff can find them.
