# Medical exams

## Pre-employment

1. Open **Pre-Employment** and record the candidate’s fitness exam.
2. Enter results for each test the job title requires.
3. The candidate signs the exam on screen (or through the signing link they receive).
4. When the exam is complete, it moves to **PEM Authorization** for HR.

## PEM Authorization (HR)

HR opens **PEM Authorization**, reviews the testing reports for each exam, and authorizes or holds the candidate. An exam appears here only when the OHC has finished it.

## Periodic medical

1. Open **Exam Schedule** to see who is due, based on the **Test requirements** set by the OHC admin.
2. Open **Periodic Medical** for the employee and record findings.
3. Add **Restrictions / Recommendations** when the worker needs them (for example, no work at height).
4. Record **Audiometry** hearing thresholds (dB HL) for workers in noisy areas.

## Vaccination

Record each dose under **Vaccination** so the dashboard can show who is due next.

## Trends

**Trend views** (set up by the admin) and **Test Trend Analysis** show whether an employee’s or department’s results are rising, stable, or falling over time. Use them in annual reviews and for high-exposure areas.

## OPD register

Record every OHC visit in **OPD** — complaint, treatment, and any referral. Emergencies are counted separately on the dashboard.
