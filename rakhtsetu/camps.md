# Camps

**Camps** manage outdoor and institution drives — planning, kits, attendance, and collection linkage.

## Typical camp day

1. Open the camp record (date, venue, organiser).
2. Confirm kit / bag assignment.
3. Register or check in donors as they arrive.
4. Move accepted donors into **Collections**.
5. Close the camp with counts (registered / collected / deferred).
