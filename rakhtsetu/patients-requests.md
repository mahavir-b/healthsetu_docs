# Patients & issue requests

Issue counter staff manage **patients**, hospital requests, and releasing units.

## Issue a request

1. Open **Patients** or **Requests**.
2. Find or register the patient (and requesting hospital / clinician when needed).
3. Create or open the blood request (component, group, units, urgency).
4. Complete crossmatch as required.
5. Issue reserved units and print issue documents.

## Register a patient

Open **People → Patients (RakhtSetu)** → **Add patient**. Enter name, blood group, phone, date of birth, gender, and the hospital / clinic. The **All patients**, **Sickle cell**, and **Thalassemia** tabs separate patients on regular transfusion programmes.

## Institutions

Use **People → Institutions** for partner hospitals and partner blood banks so requests are tied to the correct institution — important for returns, billing, and audits.
