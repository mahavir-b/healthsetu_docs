# QC & compliance

**Component QC**, **QC dashboard**, and compliance views help quality staff see what passed, failed, or is overdue.

## Daily habit

1. Open **QC dashboard** at start of shift.
2. Clear failed / pending component QC first.
3. Spot-check expiry and discard workflows.
4. Export or review compliance reports your licence requires.
