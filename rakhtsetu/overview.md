# RakhtSetu — blood bank

RakhtSetu is the blood bank module: from **camp and donor** through **collection, processing, testing**, to **issue** and **QC**.

## What we provide

- Donor registry and repeat-donor history
- Outdoor / in-house camps with kit tracking
- Collection and component processing
- TTI / grouping / crossmatch workflows
- Patient requests and issue to hospitals
- Component QC and compliance dashboards
- Partner hospitals and partner blood banks
