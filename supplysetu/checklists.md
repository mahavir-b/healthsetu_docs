# Daily QC & staff tasks

## Daily QC / checklists

Use **Daily QC** for fridge temperatures, cleanliness, equipment start-of-day checks, and similar SOP lists.

1. Open **Daily QC** / Checklists.
2. Complete each checkbox for today’s shift.
3. Save — exceptions should be visible to supervisors.

## Staff tasks

**Staff tasks** are assignable to-dos (restock, follow up vendor, calibrate). Clear your queue before end of shift.
