# SupplySetu — inventory & procurement

SupplySetu is the **hospital / blood bank store** module: catalog, stock, assets, vendors, purchase orders, and daily QC checklists.

## What we provide

- Inventory catalog (types, medicines, custom fields)
- Stock levels by location, transfers, put-away from PO
- Assets / equipment with documents and service contracts
- Vendor directory and document checklist
- Quotations and purchase orders (including vendor portal links)
- Daily QC / staff tasks and operations reports
