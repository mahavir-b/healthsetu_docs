# Assets & equipment

**Assets** track equipment — not consumable stock. Use this for centrifuges, refrigerators, monitors, and similar.

## What staff do

- Register a new asset (tag / asset number, location, make/brand)
- Attach calibration or AMC documents
- Link service contracts
- See which device is due for maintenance
