# Counsellor board

The **Counsellor** screen is where patient decisions on doctor recommendations are recorded — accept, decline, or defer — before courses and bills move forward.

## Typical flow

1. Open **Counsellor**.
2. Select the patient / pending recommendation.
3. Explain the procedure in the patient’s language.
4. Record **Accept** or **Decline** (refusal form when declining).
5. Accepted items can become a **procedure course**; declined items stay documented.
