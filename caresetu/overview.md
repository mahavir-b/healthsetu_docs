# CareSetu — clinic / OPD

CareSetu is the **outpatient clinic** module: patients, appointments, doctor OPD, procedures with sittings, counsellor decisions, and billing hand-off.

## What we provide

- Patient registration and chart
- Appointments, schedules, and token-style arrival
- Structured OPD visit (complaint → plan → Rx → sign)
- Specialty templates (e.g. General OPD, Dermatology)
- Procedure courses with sittings, consent, and photos
- Counsellor board for accept / decline recommendations
- Visit billing lines and print summaries
- Desktop document sync inbox (where enabled)
