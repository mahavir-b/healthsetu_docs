# FAQ & troubleshooting

## Signing in

**I was given a temporary password. Why am I asked to change it?**  
Temporary passwords must be changed at first sign-in. Enter the temporary one, then your new password twice.

**The sign-in page shows a different organisation name.**  
You are on the wrong URL. Use the link your administrator gave you (`yourorg.…`).

**My invite link does not work.**  
Invite links expire for safety. Ask your admin to use **Resend invite** on your user.

## Menus and access

**A screen my colleague has is missing from my menu.**  
Your role does not include it. Ask your organisation admin to check your roles. It is not a bug.

**A screen says “Loading…” or “Opening SupplySetu…” and never finishes.**  
Refresh the page. If it still does not load, sign out and in again, then tell your admin which screen and the time it happened.

**I see a red “HTTP 500” message in the corner.**  
The server could not complete a request. Retry once. If it repeats, note the screen and time and tell support. Do not re-enter the same payment or record twice without checking it was not saved.

## CareSetu

**Reception sees no free slots for a doctor.**  
The doctor has no schedule for that weekday, or is on leave. Check **Schedules**.

**The printed note says “DRAFT – NOT VALID”.**  
The doctor has not signed the visit. Open the OPD visit and choose **Sign**.

**The letterhead shows only our name, no address.**  
Fill the address and phone in the organisation **Profile**.

## SupplySetu

**Stock is empty even though goods arrived.**  
Finish stock intake and put-away on the purchase order. Stock also needs a catalog item and a location first.

**I can’t find an asset in Request inventory.**  
Request inventory lists consumable stock only. Assets are managed under **Assets**.

## RakhtSetu

**A component is not showing as available stock.**  
It is still waiting on an earlier step. Check the Processing tabs (pending separation, supervisor approval, doctor approval, pending testing) and the **Approvals** screen.

## HRSetu

**My salary slip is not in My HR.**  
Slips appear only after HR publishes the payroll run for that month.

**Check-in says I am not cleared.**  
A work clearance is pending on your record. Ask HR.

## Still stuck?

Ask your organisation administrator first. When contacting support, include the screen name, the time, and what you clicked — but never passwords or patient identifiers.
