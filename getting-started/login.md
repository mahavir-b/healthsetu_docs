# Sign in & find your way

## Sign in

1. Open the URL your administrator gave you (often `yourorg.…` or a link from email).
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. If you were asked to set a password from an invite email, use that link first — it expires for safety.

<div class="callout callout--warn">
  Never share your password. On a shared counter PC, always sign out when you leave.
</div>

## After login: where am I?

| Area | What you do here |
|------|------------------|
| **Home / Dashboard** | Snapshot of today’s work |
| **My Work / Inbox** | Tasks, approvals, messages waiting on you |
| **People** | Patients, donors, employees (depending on modules) |
| **Clinical / Operations** | Day-to-day screens for your job |
| **Administration** | Setup only — usually managers and admins |

The left menu collapses on smaller screens. Tap the menu icon to open it again.

## Common first problems

| Problem | What to try |
|---------|-------------|
| “Wrong password” | Use Forgot password if enabled, or ask admin to resend invite |
| Menu looks empty | Your role may not have capabilities yet — ask organisation admin |
| Page says retry / couldn’t load | Check network; ask admin if the organisation database is ready |
| Opened the wrong module | Use the left menu — do not bookmark an old deep link from another role |
