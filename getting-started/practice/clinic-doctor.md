# Hands-on: Doctor

**Role:** `clinic_doctor`  
**Sign in at:** `https://{your-org}.healthsetu.com/`  
**Login:** your doctor login  
**Time:** about 20–30 minutes

<div class="callout callout--tip">
  Sign out of any other account first. Work only in the staff portal. Follow the <strong>left menu</strong> and on-screen buttons (Add, Edit, Save, Refresh) — you do not need to type web addresses.
</div>

## You will learn

- Open your queue
- Complete one visit (note + Rx)
- Browse procedures
- Confirm fee masters are not editable

## Step by step

1. [ ] Sign in on the staff portal
2. [ ] In the **left menu**, open **Doctor desk** (or **OPD**)
3. [ ] In the queue, click the patient who is waiting (ask Reception to book someone if the list is empty)
4. [ ] On the visit screen, type a short clinical note (e.g. URI — symptomatic care)
5. [ ] Add a medicine (e.g. paracetamol 500 mg) → click **Save**
6. [ ] Click **Finish** / **Sign** if your clinic uses sign-off
7. [ ] Left menu → **Procedures** — look at the board (only start a sitting if your trainer says so)
8. [ ] Left menu → **Reports** — open one report you are allowed to see
9. [ ] Try left menu → **Fee items** — you should **not** be able to change prices (menu hidden or read-only)
10. [ ] Sign out (profile / account menu)

## Where to click in the UI

- Left menu → **Doctor desk** or **OPD**
- Left menu → **Procedures**
- Left menu → **Reports**
- Row / card → open patient → visit workspace → **Save** / **Sign**

## Done when

- [ ] You finished every step without an unexplained error banner
- [ ] You know which left-menu items are yours (and which are hidden)
