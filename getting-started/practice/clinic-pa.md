# Hands-on: Physician assistant (PA)

**Role:** `clinic_pa`  
**Practise on:** `https://{your-org}.healthsetu.com/`  
**Time:** about 20 minutes

<div class="callout callout--tip">
  Sign out of any other account first. Use only the staff portal — not a module Static Web App URL.
  Skip this path if your organisation did not enable the module for this role.
</div>

## You will learn

- Draft visit notes for a doctor to sign
- Support triage and clinical documentation
- Capture services without final doctor sign-off powers

## Step by step

1. [ ] Sign in at `https://{your-org}.healthsetu.com/`
2. [ ] Open OPD / doctor desk queue (`/clinic/opd` or `/clinic/doctor-desk`)
3. [ ] Open a waiting patient visit
4. [ ] Draft chief complaint / HPI / notes (do not final-sign if only the doctor may sign)
5. [ ] Capture a service or form if your clinic uses them
6. [ ] Confirm the doctor can review and sign your draft
7. [ ] Confirm you cannot edit fee masters
8. [ ] Sign out

## Screens this role uses

- `/clinic/opd`
- `/clinic/doctor-desk`
- `/clinic/patients/person/:id`

## Done when

- [ ] You finished every step without an unexplained error banner
- [ ] You know which sidebar items are yours (and which are not)
