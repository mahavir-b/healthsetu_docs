# Hands-on: Clinic cashier

**Role:** `clinic_cashier`  
**Sign in at:** `https://{your-org}.healthsetu.com/`  
**Login:** your login for this role  
**Time:** about 15 minutes

<div class="callout callout--tip">
  Sign out of any other account first. Work only in the staff portal. Follow the <strong>left menu</strong> and on-screen buttons (Add, Edit, Save, Refresh) — you do not need to type web addresses.
</div>

## You will learn

- Open billing
- Charge a consult
- Mark paid

## Step by step

1. [ ] Sign in
2. [ ] Left menu → **Billing** (Care billing) — if that item is missing, open **Cashier desk** under Billing module instead
3. [ ] Find the patient who finished OPD
4. [ ] Add charge **New consultation** (and Dressing if done)
5. [ ] Click collect / **Mark paid** → choose **Cash** (or UPI if set up)
6. [ ] Confirm receipt or Paid status
7. [ ] Confirm you cannot edit doctor schedules
8. [ ] Sign out

## Where to click in the UI

- Left menu → **Billing** or **Cashier desk**
- Bill → add charge → **Collect** / **Mark paid**

## Done when

- [ ] You finished every step without an unexplained error banner
- [ ] You know which left-menu items are yours (and which are hidden)
