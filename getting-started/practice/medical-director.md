# Hands-on: Medical director

**Role:** `medical_director`  
**Sign in at:** `https://{your-org}.healthsetu.com/`  
**Login:** your login for this role  
**Time:** about 25 minutes

<div class="callout callout--tip">
  Sign out of any other account first. Work only in the staff portal. Follow the <strong>left menu</strong> and on-screen buttons (Add, Edit, Save, Refresh) — you do not need to type web addresses.
</div>

## You will learn

- Institutions
- Equipment
- Workflows

## Step by step

1. [ ] Sign in
2. [ ] Left menu → **RakhtSetu dashboard** and **Audit and compliance**
3. [ ] Left menu → **Institutions** → **Add** or open one → **Save**
4. [ ] Left menu → **Equipment configuration** — review / save
5. [ ] Left menu → **Workflows** — open donor questionnaire; **Approve** only if you are the approver
6. [ ] Browse **QC dashboard** / **Requests**
7. [ ] Do **not** click **Delete data (RakhtSetu)** unless instructed
8. [ ] Sign out

## Where to click in the UI

- **Institutions** → **Add**
- **Equipment configuration**
- **Workflows**
- **Audit and compliance**

## Done when

- [ ] You finished every step without an unexplained error banner
- [ ] You know which left-menu items are yours (and which are hidden)
