# Users & roles

Organisation admins decide **who can sign in** and **what they can do**.

## Add a user

1. Open organisation **Users**.
2. Choose **Add user**.
3. Enter name, email, and role.
4. Save — the person gets an invite / set-password path (depending on your settings).
5. Confirm they can open only the modules they need.

## Roles vs capabilities

- A **role** is a named bundle (Reception, Doctor, Lab tech, Store).
- **Capabilities** are the fine permissions inside that role.
- Changing a role for someone already working mid-shift can hide their screens — prefer after hours.

## Disable access

When someone leaves:

1. Open their user record.
2. Disable / remove access (confirm the dialog).
3. Do not reuse their email for a different person without clearing the old account.

<div class="callout callout--warn">
  Disabling access is intentional and immediate. Confirm the name before you proceed.
</div>
