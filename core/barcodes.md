# Barcodes & labels

Shared barcode templates live in Core so every module prints the same style of label.

## Typical client uses

- Blood unit / component labels (RakhtSetu)
- Stock and asset labels (SupplySetu)
- Sample or kit labels where mapped

## How staff print (day to day)

1. Open the clinical or inventory screen that offers **Print barcode / label**.
2. Confirm the template and fields look right on the preview.
3. Print to the label printer configured for that counter.

## How admins maintain templates

1. Open organisation **Barcodes**.
2. Pick a template → **Mapping** (which screens / fields).
3. Open **Designer** to move text and barcode symbols.
4. Save, then test-print one label on the real printer.
